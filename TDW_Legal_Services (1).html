Practice Areas

How we can help you.

From traffic matters to landlord-tenant disputes, Marc brings deep experience and a proven track record to every file he handles.

01

Traffic & Highway Offences

Speeding tickets, careless driving, licence suspensions, and HTA violations. We fight to protect your driving record and keep your insurance rates down.

02

Landlord & Tenant

Representation at the Landlord and Tenant Board for both landlords and tenants. Evictions, rent disputes, maintenance issues, and lease terminations.

03

Small Claims Court

Experienced advocacy for claims up to $35,000. We prepare your case, handle filings, and represent you in court so you don't face it alone.

04

Employment Matters

Wrongful dismissal, unpaid wages, and workplace disputes before the Ministry of Labour. We help employees understand and enforce their rights.

05

Regulatory Offences

Provincial offences, licensing matters, and regulatory compliance. We navigate the system so you can focus on running your life and business.

06

General Legal Advice

Not sure where to start? Book a free consultation and we'll help you understand your options, your rights, and the best path forward.
